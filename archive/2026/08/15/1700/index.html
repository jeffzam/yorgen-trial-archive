      1. April 2017 · reported testimony

        The alleged approach

        Assistant Police Commissioner Keith Arnaud told jurors that Melvin Theuma said Yorgen Fenech invited him to the Blue Elephant restaurant around three weeks before the election was announced. Theuma alleged that the murder proposal began there.

        Read the reported testimony →
        2017
      2. April 2017 · Busy Bee, Msida

        A price is relayed

        According to the accounts reported in court, Theuma met Alfred Degiorgio, relayed the proposal and later told Fenech the requested price was €150,000. These remain allegations in the case against Fenech.

        Follow the reconstructed sequence →
        APR
      3. 1 May–4 June 2017

        Election pause, then reactivation

        Theuma testified that the plan was suspended when the snap election was called. He said Fenech told him to resume it on the Sunday the election result emerged; testimony placed the €30,000 deposit after the election.

        See the 2017 sequence →

      4. June–October 2017

        Surveillance and a changed method

        Vince Muscat later described months spent watching Caruana Galizia’s movements. His account said an earlier firearm plan gave way to a remotely detonated car bomb.

        Read Muscat’s reported account →
        JUN

      5. 16 October 2017 · 2:58pm

        Daphne Caruana Galizia is assassinated

        A bomb placed beneath the driver’s seat of her rented car exploded shortly after she left her Bidnija home. The 53-year-old journalist was killed.

        Read MaltaToday’s retrospective →

      6. 4–5 December 2017

        First arrests and murder charges

        Ten men were arrested in coordinated raids. George and Alfred Degiorgio and Vince Muscat were charged the following day with murdering Caruana Galizia; all three initially pleaded not guilty.

        Read the 2017 court report →
        DEC
      7. 14–30 November 2019

        The investigation reaches Fenech

        Theuma was arrested on 14 November and agreed to cooperate. Fenech was intercepted aboard his yacht on 20 November; Theuma received a conditional pardon on 25 November. On 30 November, Fenech was charged and pleaded not guilty.

        Vince Muscat admits his role

        Muscat pleaded guilty to all six heads of indictment and was sentenced to 15 years in prison after supplying information to investigators.

      9. 14 October 2022

        The Degiorgio brothers plead guilty

        George and Alfred Degiorgio changed their pleas on the first day of their jury trial. Each received a 40-year prison sentence for the assassination.

        A jury found Robert Agius and Jamie Vella guilty of complicity in the assassination and related offences. They were later sentenced to life imprisonment.
