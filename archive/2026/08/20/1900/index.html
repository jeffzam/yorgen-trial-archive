      One recording.

      3layers kept
      distinct
      Recorded account

      Allegations and denial

      Fenech denied ordering the murder and alleged Schembri was responsible. He named Muscat, Vella and Cremona as people he said learned of Schembri’s claimed role after the killing.

      Money account

      Broad totals, contested purpose

      Zahra said Fenech’s and Theuma’s reported overall figures broadly aligned around €400,000–€450,000. That does not settle why the money was paid or who was responsible.

      Layer 01 · allegationWhat Fenech told police

      A recorded account with claims about Schembri, Muscat, Vella, Cremona, Theuma, money and an alleged escape plan.

      Layer 02 · checkWhat Zahra said police tested

      Messages, burner-phone activity, threat reports, recordings and statements by other people were put alongside the interview.

      Layer 03 · boundaryWhat the court allowed

      The inspector could explain investigative work and facts, but could not make submissions, interpret the evidence or decide credibility.

      The jury decides evidentiary weight
      Investigative check

      Accounts did not simply merge

      Vella and Schembri denied an alleged escape arrangement. Vella confirmed a legal-team plan but, Zahra said, described documents rather than the “script” alleged by Fenech.

      Judicial limit

      Facts, not argument

      Judge Edwina Grima ruled that Zahra could describe his work but must not offer opinions or weigh the evidence. The recorded words remain for jurors to assess.
